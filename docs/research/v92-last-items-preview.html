Rules

  • Saves only what the customer actually said. It never guesses or fills a field “to be safe”.
  • If the customer changes their mind (“make it ₹20,000”), the AI updates its own value.
  • A value someone on your team typed or changed is never overwritten by the AI.
  • It's done as part of the reply the AI is already writing, so no extra message goes out.
  • The fields depend on the business: everyone gets Wants · Budget · Needed by · City/area. Real estate adds BHK · Needs a loan · Site visit. Courses add Course · Preferred batch. Answers to your own “Questions to ask every customer” (Anril Setup › Questions & scoring) are saved too.
  • Shows in Chats › About and on the lead's page in Inbound leads (screen 32 in the pack), and in the Inbound leads download.

What changes in the code

  • New AI tool save_customer_details in backend/app/services/ai_reply.py. It sits next to send_quote and the other tools.
  • Values go into the existing leads.collected_data. Migration 340 adds leads.detail_sources (for each field: AI or a staff member, and when), so the “from chat” and “by Karthik” marks and the never-overwrite rule work.
  • New route PATCH /leads/{id}/details for the ✎ edit. Tenant-checked and logged in the Activity log.
  • Frontend: components/chats/CustomerActions.tsx (About panel) and components/lead-details-panel.tsx (the lead's page).
  • Tests: the tool saves, refuses to overwrite a staff edit, and ignores empty values; plus the edit route.

Rules

  • The 👍 is only on AI agent replies, not on messages your team sent.
  • Liking the same reply twice doesn't save it twice.
  • The AI uses your 10 newest examples, so a long list doesn't slow it down or cost more. Up to 50 can be saved.
  • Examples teach style only. If an example says “₹2,000 COD” and the Catalogue says something else, the Catalogue wins.
  • Remove takes an example out straight away. ↶ Undo last change on Anril Setup brings it back.

What changes in the code

  • Migration 340 lets ai_teach_items store a third kind, liked_reply (today only answer and word). It also adds message_id, so the same reply can't be saved twice.
  • The prompt in ai_reply.py gets a short “reply in this style” block made from the 10 newest examples.
  • Routes on the existing teach API: like a message, add, list, remove.
  • Frontend: a 👍 on AI bubbles in the Chats thread, and the real card in app/dashboard/teach/page.tsx in place of the “coming soon” one.
  • Tests: like saves once, a team message can't be liked, prompt holds at most 10, another business's message is refused.

Three things to decide before I build

  1. Who can 👍 a reply? My pick: the owner and managers, the same people who can use “Teach after reply”. The other choice is everyone on the team.
  2. Details as columns and filters in Inbound leads (Budget, Wants…), as the pack's screen 32 note says. My pick: later. The About panel, the lead's page and the download come first.
  3. Should the AI update a detail when the customer changes their mind? My pick: yes, but only its own values, never one your team typed.
