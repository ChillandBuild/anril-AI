What AiSensy has that Anril doesn't

Every AiSensy feature I saw in their logged-in app, checked against Anril's code. Only real gaps are listed in full, plus a short note on where Anril is already ahead.

How this was checked. AiSensy features come from clicking through every sidebar section of a logged-in account (Free plan, number not yet approved). Anril was checked by searching our code (backend/app/routes, frontend/app/dashboard). "Missing" means I found no code for it. "Partly" means something close exists but not the same thing. A feature hidden behind a paid plan on either side may be missed.

Top 5 to build first

1 · small effortAI template writer

Prompt → 3 drafts → "Use this", inside our existing template builder. Plus a ready-made seasonal template library.

2 · medium effortWhatsApp Forms (real Meta Flows)

Form screens inside WhatsApp, started from a template button or an ad click. AiSensy only half-does this, so we could get ahead.

3 · medium effortWebsite chat widget + link/QR maker

Simple lead sources that every competitor gives away free.

4 · medium effortShopify / WooCommerce / Sheets

Direct connections for cart recovery and contact sync, not only our generic webhook.

5 · large effortCreate Meta ads with AI

The most impressive AiSensy feature, but it needs Meta ads permissions, app review and handling real ad money.

Where Anril is already ahead

I found none of these in AiSensy's app. They're worth leading with in sales:

  • Telecalling: calling cockpit, call recordings with AI review, a telecaller call-first list.
  • Lead scoring (hot, warm, cold) and lead assignment.
  • Deeper Meta ad reports: ad-to-lead journey and ad advice, beyond CTR and CPC.
  • SMS with DLT, OTP, Instagram and Telegram channels.
  • Orders with GST invoices sent on WhatsApp.
  • An operator console for running many clients.
