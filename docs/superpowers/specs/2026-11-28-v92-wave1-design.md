# v9.2 leftovers, wave 1 — Dial a number checks, Excel "Skip or update", Archived & blocked together

Approved by Keerthi on 2026-11-28. Source: v9.2 design pack screens 231, 165, 210; leftovers decision page
(https://claude.ai/artifact/9b99KRxFFkw28tN39qJ9YU), items P88, P34, P19.

## Why
The v9.2 design pack is mostly built; these three were half built. Keerthi picked them first, with one rule:
build them into the pages that already exist, never as a new page or tab.

## Goals
- **P88 — Call IQ › Calling › Manual Dial.** Before pressing Call, the person sees who the number is, whether it
  may be called, how often it was called today, which number the call goes from, and what they dialled today.
- **P34 — WhatsApp › Templates › Excel drawer.** A row whose name + language already exists can be updated on
  Meta instead of always being skipped.
- **P19 — Chats.** Archived and blocked chats on one "Archived & blocked" tab again (as before 21 Nov, `7d50d0a5`),
  laid out like screen 210.

## Non-goals / anti-goals
- No new page, route or tab for any of the three (anti-goal: the same job split across two places).
- No fake checks: the national DND register is not available to us, so it shows "Not checked", never a green OK.
- No number picker: Prem hid the per-person SIM/office choice until the telecaller phone app (held #16).
- The dial screen must never disagree with what the Call button does: the checks come from the same server
  rules (`pre_dial_block`, do_not_call, opted_out).

## Approaches considered
1. **Extend the existing panels (picked).** Manual Dial's empty right pane shows the checks; the Excel review row
   gets a Skip / Update choice; Chats' Archived view gets the blocked card beside it.
2. A separate "Dial a number" sub-page like screen 231 — rejected by Keerthi (no new page or tab).
3. A separate "update existing templates" upload — rejected: the same job in two places.

## Design
**P88**
- Left panel (number pad + customer matches) unchanged; under it one line "Calling from: Office number" or
  "Your phone (SIM)" from the caller's effective calling mode.
- While Manual Dial is open and no lead is picked, the right pane shows:
  Found in Customers (match for the typed number, Open customer ›); Before you call — Not on Said stop,
  Not marked Do not call, National DND list "Not checked", Calling hours (only when the rule is on; OK · now or
  "Outside hours"), Called N times today already (warning, Last: time); a note on what happens to a new number;
  Dialled today by you (time, name or masked number, outcome).
- Server: one read-only check for a typed number (same rules as the Call button), and today's calls by this
  caller. A brand-new number dialled from the pad is saved with "came from: dialled by <caller name>".
- Hidden numbers stay hidden for callers who must not see them.

**P34**
- Row status "exists": "Already exists. Skip or update?" with Skip (default) / Update.
- Update sends the row as an edit of the existing template through the same edit path as the Templates page
  (`PATCH /templates/{id}` rules: only Approved / Rejected / Paused can be edited; Meta caps approved edits at
  1 per 24 h and 10 per 30 days). A refused edit shows Meta's reason on that row.
- Send button counts updates ("Send 5 to Meta · 1 update"). The drawer's note no longer says "never changed".

**P19**
- One tab "Archived & blocked" replaces the Archived and Blocked tabs.
- Archived table (filters, Move back, export) on the left; "Blocked · N" card on the right (Unblock, "+ N more"
  expands in place, export), and the "Blocked is not Said stop" card under it. Stacked on phones.
